About

I am a geophysicist and data scientist interested in Earth processes that shape, and are shaped by, the climate system. I currently work at KoBold Metals, where we pair geoscience data with machine learning to explore for the critical minerals needed for the energy transition.

Before moving into mineral exploration, my work focused on the fluid dynamics and solid mechanics of water and ice as Earth materials. I have used geophysical methods, such as ice-penetrating radar to survey glaciers and ice sheets, or magnetic resonance to survey subsurface water.

I believe in and practice open science, with free access to data, code, and manuscripts. Most of my research software is freely available, and PDFs for all of my publications are linked below.
